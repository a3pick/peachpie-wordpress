=== Media Sweeper Pro ===
Requires at least: 5.3
Requires PHP: 7.2
Stable tag: 1.0.0
License: GPLv2 or later

Find unused images/videos/audio, preview them in bulk, quarantine or delete them, remove orphan files,
detect duplicates and convert JPG/PNG to WebP while safely rewriting every reference.

== Notes ==
* Take a full backup (files + database) before large operations.
* Quarantine is reversible; permanent deletion is not.
* WP-CLI: wp msw scan | stats | list | quarantine | delete | restore | convert | purge-originals
